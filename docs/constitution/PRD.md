This document states the objective: the WHAT and the horizon. It is
generalist, holistic, top-down, and short — every line is a standing context
cost, and each fact lives with its owner, reached by wikilink.

The template ships it empty on purpose. Filling it — a few paragraphs naming
what the product is, who it serves, and where it is going — is among the
first acts of a cloned project, together with picking the code-root pair
([[HARNESS]]). Its keeper is the PRD guardian, `docs/agents/guardian-prd.md`.
